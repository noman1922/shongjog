# Shongjog

**Shongjog** is a web-based Student-Alumni Career Networking and Opportunity Platform that connects university students and alumni to build professional relationships, discover internships and jobs, share achievements, and grow their careers.

## Project Overview

Many students struggle to find career guidance and professional opportunities because they have limited connections with experienced alumni. Shongjog aims to bridge this gap by creating a trusted university-based networking platform where students and alumni can connect, communicate, and collaborate.

## Features

### Student
- Register and login
- Create and manage professional profile
- Search alumni
- Filter alumni by university, department, company, and skills
- Send and manage connection requests
- Create professional posts
- Share projects and achievements
- View news feed
- Like and comment on posts
- View internship and job opportunities
- Show internship availability

### Alumni
- Register and login
- Manage professional profile
- Search students
- Discover talented students
- Share career experiences
- Post internships
- Post job vacancies
- Connect with students
- Like and comment on posts

### Admin
- Secure admin dashboard
- Manage users
- Monitor posts and activities
- Remove inappropriate content
- Manage reports
- Restrict or suspend accounts
- Monitor internship and job postings
